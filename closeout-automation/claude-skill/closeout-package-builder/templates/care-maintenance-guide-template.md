## Hardwood / Engineered Hardwood
**Daily/weekly care:** Sweep or vacuum (hard-floor setting, no beater
bar) regularly; damp-mop only, never wet-mop.
**Avoid:** Standing water, steam mops, high heels/cleats, direct sunlight
without UV-protective window treatment, humidity swings outside 35–55%.
**Safe products:** pH-neutral hardwood cleaners recommended by the
manufacturer. **Avoid:** vinegar/water mixes, oil soaps, wax-based
products (they can void the finish warranty).
**Call us if:** boards start cupping/crowning (often a moisture issue —
catch it early), finish is peeling, or you hear new squeaking that wasn't
there at handover.

## Luxury Vinyl Plank / Tile (LVP/LVT)
**Daily/weekly care:** Sweep or vacuum, damp-mop with manufacturer-approved
cleaner.
**Avoid:** Direct, prolonged sun exposure (can cause discoloration/
expansion), dragging heavy furniture without floor protectors, rubber-
backed mats (can discolor the surface).
**Safe products:** Manufacturer-approved LVP cleaner. **Avoid:** wax,
polish, or abrasive cleaners.
**Call us if:** planks start lifting at seams or you notice gapping.

## Ceramic/Porcelain Tile
**Daily/weekly care:** Sweep/vacuum, mop with pH-neutral tile cleaner.
**Avoid:** Acidic cleaners (vinegar, lemon) on grout, dragging heavy
objects that can chip edges.
**Safe products:** pH-neutral tile & grout cleaner. Reseal grout per the
manufacturer's recommended interval (commonly every 1–2 years).
**Call us if:** tiles crack, sound hollow when tapped, or grout crumbles.

## Carpet
**Daily/weekly care:** Vacuum at least weekly (more in high-traffic
areas), blot spills immediately (don't rub).
**Avoid:** Steam cleaning with harsh chemicals before checking
manufacturer guidance, walking on wet carpet with shoes.
**Safe products:** Manufacturer-recommended spot cleaner; professional
steam cleaning roughly every 12–18 months.
**Call us if:** seams start separating or you notice matting/traffic
patterns forming unusually early.
