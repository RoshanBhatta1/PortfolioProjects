# Flooring Contractor Closeout Checklist (Canada)

Use this as the source of truth for what "done" means on a flooring
project closeout. Mirrors the checklist built into the ClosoutHQ web app
(`web/src/lib/checklist-template.ts`) — keep the two in sync if you edit
either.

## Compliance & Permits
- [ ] **Building/renovation permit closed** (final inspection passed) —
  only required if the scope needed a municipal permit.
- [ ] **Certificate of Substantial Performance / Completion signed** —
  triggers the statutory holdback release timeline under the provincial
  Construction Act.

## Site Verification & Testing
- [ ] **Subfloor moisture test report on file** (ASTM F2170 RH or
  calcium chloride) — manufacturer warranties are void without this.
- [ ] **Subfloor prep / flatness sign-off** — confirms flatness/leveling
  tolerance was met before install.

## Warranty & Product
- [ ] **Manufacturer product warranty registered** (product, lot/batch #,
  SKU).
- [ ] **Workmanship warranty letter issued** *(AI-drafted)*.
- [ ] **Care & maintenance guide delivered** *(AI-drafted, tailored to
  flooring type)*.

## Financial & Legal
- [ ] **WSIB/WCB clearance certificate on file.**
- [ ] **Statutory declaration / lien waiver** (holdback release).
- [ ] **Final invoice issued & payment confirmed.**

## Handover & Sign-off
- [ ] **Final walkthrough deficiency list completed & resolved.**
- [ ] **Leftover material / waste disposal confirmed** (optional).
- [ ] **Before/after site photos on file.**
- [ ] **Client acceptance & sign-off form signed.**
- [ ] **Closeout package cover letter drafted** *(AI-drafted)*.
- [ ] **Final closeout package delivered to client.**
