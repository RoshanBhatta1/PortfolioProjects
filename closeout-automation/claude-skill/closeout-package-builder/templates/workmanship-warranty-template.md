**Subject: Workmanship Warranty — {{project_address}}**

Dear {{client_name}},

Thank you for choosing {{contractor_name}} for your flooring project at
{{project_address}}, completed on {{completion_date}}.

This letter confirms our workmanship warranty on the installation of
{{flooring_types}}, separate from the manufacturer's product warranty
(registered separately — see your closeout package for details).

**Warranty period:** {{warranty_years}} year(s) from the completion date
above.

**What's covered:**
- Installation defects such as gaps, seams, or transitions that occurred
  due to installation error
- Squeaks or movement traceable to installation workmanship, not
  subfloor or structural issues outside our scope

**What's not covered:**
- Normal wear and tear
- Moisture or humidity damage occurring after handover (e.g. plumbing
  leaks, HVAC/humidity control issues on the client's side)
- Manufacturer product defects — these are covered under the separate
  product warranty; contact us and we'll help you file that claim too
- Damage from furniture, pets, improper cleaning products, or anything
  outside normal residential/commercial use

**How to file a claim:** Contact us at {{contractor_contact}} with a
description and photos of the issue. We'll schedule an inspection within
a reasonable timeframe.

Thank you again for your business — we're glad to stand behind this
installation.

Sincerely,
{{contractor_name}}
